# 1. Import Clearance Process Through Seaports in Bangladesh

## Overview

The import clearance process through Bangladesh seaports involves a structured sequence of activities from vessel arrival to final cargo release.

### Key Process Steps

1. Pre-arrival preparation  
2. Submission of import documents  
3. Cargo manifest submission  
4. Vessel arrival and documentation  
5. Bill of Entry submission  
6. Importer verification and data entry  
7. Customs duty and tax assessment  
8. Duty payment  
9. Physical inspection  
10. Laboratory testing where required  
11. Customs clearance approval  
12. Out-pass collection  
13. Port charge payment  
14. Cargo release  
15. Transportation to final destination  
16. Customs audit and final release

## Key Documents

- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Bill of Lading
- Letter of Credit
- Bill of Entry
- Insurance Documents
- Import Registration Certificate
- Customs and Port Documents

## Professional Insight

Efficient import clearance requires coordination between:

Importer → C&F Agent → Shipping Agent → Customs → Port Authority → Bank → Transporter → Warehouse

Digital documentation and ERP integration can significantly reduce delays, duplication and manual errors.

# 3. Letter of Credit and Trade Finance

## Overview

A Letter of Credit is one of the most important financial instruments used in international trade.

It reduces payment risk between buyer and seller by involving banks in the transaction.

## Basic LC Process

1. Buyer contacts seller  
2. Buyer approaches issuing bank  
3. Issuing bank opens the LC  
4. LC is transmitted to advising bank  
5. Advising bank informs seller  
6. Seller prepares and ships goods  
7. Seller submits required documents  
8. Advising bank reviews documents  
9. Documents are sent to issuing bank  
10. Issuing bank verifies compliance  
11. Payment or acceptance is processed  
12. Buyer receives documents and clears goods

## Common Documents

- Trade Licence
- TIN
- VAT Registration
- IRC
- Proforma Invoice
- Insurance Cover Note
- Bank Documents
- Supplier Information

## Professional Insight

An effective LC process requires strong coordination between:

Buyer → Seller → Issuing Bank → Advising Bank → Shipping Company → Customs

ERP and digital trade finance systems can significantly improve transaction visibility and document control.

- Bottles and cans

- Organic waste

Create scheduled waste collection routes and SOPs.

Build a structured reverse-logistics network.

- CCTV

- Data dashboards
